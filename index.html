The Dry Eye Center at New Path Vision

Dry eye has different causes. Your treatment should reflect yours.

Burning, grittiness, watering, fluctuating vision and contact-lens discomfort can come from different problems. Our dry-eye evaluation looks at the tear film, eyelid oil glands and ocular surface — so treatment is based on what we actually find, not just another bottle of drops.
