Dry Eye Center

Dry eye symptoms look different from person to person.

Burning, watering, grittiness, and blurry or fluctuating vision can all be signs of dry eye — even when they seem to contradict each other.

Schedule a Dry Eye Evaluation Back to Dry Eye Center

Common symptoms

Burning or stinging

A persistent irritated feeling, especially by evening.

Watery eyes

Excess tearing is a common reflex response to dryness.

Gritty or sandy feeling

Like there's something in your eye that isn't there.

Fluctuating vision

Vision that blurs and clears as you blink.

Contact lens discomfort

Lenses that used to feel fine but don't anymore.

Light sensitivity

Increased discomfort in bright light.

Redness

Visible irritation, especially by end of day.

Eye fatigue

Eyes that feel tired, especially during screen use.

What causes it?

Dry eye can come from your tear glands, your oil glands, or inflammation on the eye's surface — often more than one at once.
