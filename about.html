About New Path Vision

Fewer patients per day, not fewer answers.

We built this practice around one idea: a visit shouldn't feel like a conveyor belt. One ophthalmologist, four optometrists, and an in-house Dry Eye Center mean you see the right person, get a straight answer, and never feel rushed out the door.

Why we started New Path Vision

Pottstown didn't need another practice that books you every ten minutes and hands you off to whoever's free. It needed one where the ophthalmologist and optometrists actually talk to each other about your case — and where chronic dry eye gets treated as its own condition, not an afterthought at the end of a routine exam.

That's the practice we run. Full-scope family eye care lives alongside a dedicated Dry Eye Center, so whether you're in for glasses, a cataract consult, or years of drops that never worked, you're seeing people with the time to actually look into it.
