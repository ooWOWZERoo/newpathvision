Comprehensive Eye Exams

An eye exam should tell you more than your prescription.

A full-scope check of your vision and eye health — not just a quick read of your prescription.

Book now Call the office

What a comprehensive exam can include

Visual acuity

How clearly you see at distance and up close.

Refraction

Your exact glasses or contact lens prescription.

Eye pressure check

A key screening measure for glaucoma risk.

Retinal health check

A look at the back of the eye for early signs of disease.

What to bring

Your current glasses or contacts, a list of medications, your insurance card, and sunglasses for the ride home if you're due for dilation.

What to expect

Plan for about an hour. You'll be seen by one of our optometrists, with our ophthalmologist available for anything that needs a closer look.

Frequently asked questions

How often should I get an eye exam?

General guidance is every 1-2 years for adults without known eye conditions — your doctor will recommend a schedule based on your eyes.

Will my eyes be dilated?

Not always. Our retinal imaging technology may reduce the need for dilation at some visits — your doctor will recommend it when it's clinically necessary.

Can I book an eye exam and an eyewear fitting the same day?

Ask when you book — we'll do our best to combine visits.
