Cataract Evaluations

Cloudy vision, glare, or trouble driving at night? It may be time to evaluate for cataracts.

Straight answers on what's next, evaluated and co-managed with our on-staff ophthalmologist.

Book now (opens in a new tab) Call the office

Symptoms worth evaluating

Cloudy or blurry vision

Especially if glasses don't sharpen it anymore.

Glare or halos at night

Trouble with headlights or bright lights.

Trouble driving at night

Vision that feels noticeably worse after dark.

Fading or yellowing colors

Colors that seem less vivid than they used to.

What happens if surgery is recommended

Our on-staff ophthalmologist evaluates whether cataracts are affecting your vision enough to warrant surgery, and walks you through what that process looks like and what New Path Vision's role is before and after the procedure.

Frequently asked questions

Does having cataracts mean I need surgery right away?

Not necessarily — many cataracts are monitored before surgery is recommended. Your evaluation will tell you where things stand.

What does New Path Vision do versus the surgeon?

We evaluate and monitor your cataracts, and coordinate your care before and after surgery.
