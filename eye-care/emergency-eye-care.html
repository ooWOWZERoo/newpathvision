Urgent

Sudden eye problem? Call us now.

For sudden vision loss, severe eye pain, a chemical injury, or a serious eye injury, call us immediately or seek emergency medical care. This page is general guidance, not a substitute for a clinical evaluation.

Call 610-326-2754 Now

Symptoms that warrant urgent evaluation

Sudden vision loss

Even if it's brief or in one eye.

Severe eye pain

Especially with light sensitivity or nausea.

Flashes or new floaters

Especially a sudden shower of new floaters.

Chemical exposure or injury

Any eye injury or exposure to a chemical.

When to call 911 or go to the ER instead

A chemical splash, a penetrating eye injury, or vision loss alongside other stroke symptoms (face drooping, slurred speech, weakness) needs emergency medical care right away — call 911 or go to the nearest ER rather than waiting for a callback.

What not to do for a common injury

Don't rub the eye, don't try to remove an embedded object yourself, and for a chemical splash, rinse with clean water immediately and continue while seeking care.
